Admission Procedure

Admission procedure is based on the guidelines of CBSE. Admissions are open on availability of vacancies and based on defined categories. Registration for admission into LKG/Class I, opens in the month of February / March and for other classes in the month of April / May/June.

  1. Registration/Admission form will be supplied in the school Accounts Office along with school prospectus on payment. Registration/Admission form can be filled online too.
  2. Mere registration does not guarantee admission.
  3. In the cases of admissions to Pre-Primary, prescribed age limit is compulsory. Date of birth certificate in original from competent authority (Municipal Corporation/Local Body) is necessary for admission into LKG.

Age limit for Pre-Primary : Nursery / LKG:

3 years + must be completed by 31st of May of the admitting year.

UKG:

4 years + must be completed by 31st of May of the admitting year. Certificate of promotion to UKG from previous school is compulsory.

ADMISSION will be confirmed only on submission of:

  1. Admission form: (to be supplied by the school office).
  2. Submission of Transfer Certificate / Record sheet from recognized school.
  3. Payment of prescribed fee by stipulated date.
