Our Inspiration

The school is a dream of our respected Chairman Sir who coined this inspiration through his great father Late. Shri Lavkesh Singh; former Law, Labour and Revenue Minister of MP GOVT. He was popularly called “Vikas Purush” due to his vision of bringing light of knowledge and wisdom to society.

His works are apparently bringing amenities to the whole society. We are grateful to him for his valuable contributions to Beohari. His name “ Vikas Purush” itself acclaims the vision and inspiration of establishing a school that depicts his ideals and objectives in life.
