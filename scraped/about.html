Vimla Global School

"The Future Begins Here!"

We aim at providing education- a place where students need is the centre and excellence is stressed by giving more individual attention and nurturing social development. The fundamental concept of the school is to reduce the generation gap in the entire field, by the time the students get mature and enter into the practical aspects of life.

The school aims to provide a holistic education and has comprehensive facilities to develop the child's all round personality. The classrooms are spacious and designed to be equipped with all the basic facilities. Sufficient space has been allocated for pursuing the various co-curricular activities.

Highly experienced and qualified staff.

Our Objectives

We at VGS are motivated to provide all round development based education in Beohari. We believe that good education is a basic right of every child and we ensure it here with qualified staff, good infrastructure, Smart Classes, Labs and equipments and library.
